Overview

Stripe is not a payments processor. It is financial infrastructure. Its APIs are embedded in the core payment flows of Amazon, Ford, and OpenAI, and migrating means rewriting financial plumbing, not cancelling a SaaS subscription. The business is strong. The question is price, not quality.

The objective was to determine a fair value for Stripe and to assess whether the $159B private valuation (the February 2026 tender) is a compelling entry point ahead of a potential IPO. The output is an Excel model and a nine-slide investment memorandum with a two-method blended valuation and a SELL recommendation.

Methodology

The valuation blends two independent methods at equal weight: trading comparables (50%) and a discounted cash flow (50%). The model runs three scenarios, Bear, Base and Bull, that re-run every driver from take rate to terminal growth, and 17 built-in checks confirm the model's integrity.

The DCF spans 10 years of explicit free cash flow after stock-based comp, anchored to 2025A revenue of $6.5B and $1.9T in total payment volume. WACC is 11.38%, built from a 4.44% risk-free rate (US 10Y), a beta of 1.30x, and Damodaran's 5.55% equity risk premium, with 3.0% terminal growth. It uses two terminal values, a Gordon growth method ($42.4B) and a 12x exit multiple ($54.8B), averaged to a DCF enterprise value of $48.6B.

Seven peers across three cohorts were analysed: payments infrastructure (Adyen, Block, Payoneer), payment networks as moat benchmarks (Visa, Mastercard), and e-commerce platforms (Shopify, PayPal). The quality set of Adyen, Visa, Mastercard and Shopify sets the 9.9x forward EV/Revenue Base multiple, with Adyen (5.9x) as the Bear case and Visa (12.6x) as the Bull case. Stripe's Rule of 40 score of 56% is ahead of Shopify at 47.3% and below Adyen at 70.7%.

Key findings

  • Blended enterprise value of $65.2B gives an equity value of $70.2B, 55.8% below the $159B private valuation.
  • Comps (50% weight) imply $81.8B at 9.9x forward revenue, and the DCF (50% weight) implies $48.6B.
  • The mark implies 18.7x forward revenue, 88% above the 9.9x quality-peer average. It would need a roughly 43x exit EBITDA multiple, against the 12x used in the model.
  • The call is SELL in every scenario: Bear $34.4B, Base $65.2B and Bull $99.6B are all below the $154B market-implied enterprise value. It also holds at any comps/DCF weighting, from −45% to −66%.
  • Free cash flow after SBC grows from $1.00B (2025A) to $5.09B (2034E), with adjusted EBITDA margin expanding from 29.5% to 33.4%.
  • AI agent commerce is an unmodelled free option: autonomous transactions need a programmable payments layer, and Stripe's API-first architecture is the default candidate.
